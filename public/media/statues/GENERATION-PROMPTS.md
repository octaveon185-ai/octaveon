# Statue generation prompts

Built-in ImageGen was used in `photorealistic-natural` mode. Every variant used this shared production direction:

> Create a real museum photograph of an original classical warm-white marble sculpture, never a 3D render. Preserve subtle veins, pores, tiny chips and authentic age marks. Use a soft warm key light from upper left and an extremely subtle cool fill from lower right. Produce a complete isolated vertical sculpture with correct anatomy, credible face, eyes, hands, fingers, feet, drapery and base. Place it on a perfectly flat solid `#00ff00` chroma-key background with no floor, shadow, reflection, text, symbol or watermark. Keep generous padding and do not crop any part of the sculpture.

## Selected compositions

### Sonic Alchemy

Original graceful male classical marble musician playing a plain ancestral transverse flute. Weight on the rear leg, front foot relaxed, bust turned gently right and head lowered toward the flute. A restrained mantle wraps the lower body in realistic heavy folds. Hands, fingers, flute holes and mouth contact must be anatomically convincing. Serene concentration rather than theatrical performance.

### Nourishment

Original serene feminine classical marble figure in calm welcoming contrapposto. She cradles one simple shallow ritual bowl at waist height with both anatomically correct hands clearly visible. Soft heavy drapery wraps one shoulder and the lower body. Slight torso inclination and an authoritative but gentle expression.

### Celebration

Original youthful androgynous classical figure, fully clothed in an opaque full-length chiton and mantle, in a grounded rhythmic turning step. Both arms make a restrained low sweeping gesture, hands fully visible with relaxed separated fingers. The fabric spirals around the body in heavy believable carved folds, suggesting collective movement without hiding the feet. Serene present expression.

### Closing Warrior

Original mature classical warrior seated low in a near-reclining pose. One knee rises while the other leg stretches along a complete oval base; one palm bears weight behind the torso and the free arm relaxes across the raised knee. Head leans back toward the light, eyes sculpted upward and expression transformed and at peace. Discreet helmet, simple weathered armor, realistic drapery, no weapon and no battle.
